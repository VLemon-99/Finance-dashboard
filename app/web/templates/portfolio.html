Option prices are per share; P&L, premium and capital are in dollars (100 shares per contract). Click a ticker to fold its section. Capital at risk: cash-secured put = strike × 100 × contracts − premium; covered call = stock price when sold × 100 × contracts; credit spread = (difference between strikes − net credit) × 100 × contracts; poor man's covered call = price paid for the bought call (LEAP) × 100 × contracts. A short call is covered only when you hold 100 shares per contract here, or it's linked to a LEAP. Linked pairs show their combined P&L (% of the premium for a credit spread, of the capital for a poor man's covered call); when a pair's sold option expires or is closed, the link ends. {% if view.rate %}Delta uses a risk-free rate of {{ view.rate.rate | percent(2) }}{% if view.rate.is_fallback %} (fallback from settings){% endif %}.{% endif %}
